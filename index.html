Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the following games published by Intrecci di Carta:

🐚 Cups Game  🌉 Deadly Bridge  🃏 Three Card Game  🍕 Pizza Ninja

Short version: Our games do not collect any personal data. No registration, no tracking, no personal information is ever sent to our servers — because we don't have any servers.

1. Data We Collect

Our games do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or any contact information.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not use advertising networks that collect personal data.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The games store the following data locally on your device only using browser localStorage:

  • Language preference – the language you selected (e.g. "en", "it")
  • Pro version status – whether you have unlocked the Pro version
  • Game progress – high scores and level progress

This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

3. In-App Purchases

Our games offer an optional in-app purchase (Pro version, €1.99). All payment processing is handled exclusively by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store any payment or financial information.

4. Advertising

Our games may display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob). These networks may collect certain device information in accordance with their own privacy policies. Users can remove all advertising by purchasing the Pro version.

AdMob Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

5. Children

Our games are suitable for all ages and are not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: intreccidicarta@gmail.com
